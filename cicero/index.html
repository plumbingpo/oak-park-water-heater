Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Cicero

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush every year if their water is hard.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as discolored hot water, rumbling during heating, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one after the tank lets go.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet the same sediment, neglected for a few years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and shorten the life of the tank, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or looks wrong. A quick call saves time, money, and cold showers.
